Priority Quest • Art presentation

Notan Shadow Knight Quest

Goal: understand Notan art and practice a short, confident presentation.

1. What is Notan?

Notan is a Japanese art idea about balancing light and dark. Look for shapes that mirror or balance each other.

+20 XP

2. Art Words

Practice saying: contrast, symmetry, balance, positive space, negative space.

+20 XP

3. Explain Your Art

Point to one light shape and one dark shape. Say how they help the picture feel balanced.

+25 XP

4. Brave Voice

Stand tall, look up, speak slowly, and finish with: “Thank you. Do you have any questions?”

+25 XP

Presentation Script

“Hi, my name is Phillip. My project uses Notan, which means light and dark balance. I used dark shapes and light shapes to make contrast. My favorite part is ______ because ______. Thank you.”
