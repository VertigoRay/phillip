Priority Quest - Art Presentation

Notan Shadow Knight Quest

Goal: understand Notan art words and practice a short, confident presentation.

Art Words

Contrast

Contrast means two things look very different, like black next to white.

Sentence: I made contrast by putting next to .

Symmetry

Symmetry means both sides match or almost match, like folded paper.

Sentence: My art has symmetry because .

Balance

Balance means the picture feels steady because the shapes share the space.

Sentence: The picture feels balanced because .

Positive Space

Positive space is the main shape your eyes notice first.

Sentence: My positive space is .

Negative Space

Negative space is the empty or background space around the main shape.

Sentence: My negative space is .

1. What is Notan?

Notan is a Japanese art idea about balancing light and dark shapes.

+20 XP

2. Point to the Art

Point to one light shape and one dark shape. Say how they help the picture feel balanced.

+25 XP

3. Practice With Blanks

Read the script and fill in the blank parts. Use your art words when you can.

+25 XP

4. Brave Voice

Stand tall, look up, speak slowly, and finish with: "Thank you. Do you have any questions?"

+30 XP

Presentation Practice

  1. Round 1: Say it with blanks: "My favorite part is because ."
  2. Round 2: Say it while pointing to your light and dark shapes.
  3. Round 3: Say it without looking at the page. It is okay to pause and breathe.

Presentation Script

"Hi, my name is Phillip. My project uses Notan, which means light and dark balance. I used dark shapes and light shapes to make contrast. My positive space is . My negative space is . My favorite part is because . Thank you."
